Register now Workshop schedule

We are organising a workshop series where participants will learn the practical skills needed to record, script, and edit short-form, social media-friendly science videos.

In a true learning-by-doing style, participants will work in teams and collaborate with partnering organisations to create videos for them.

To supplement the practical sessions, we will host optional discussion sessions about the changing media landscape. These sessions will examine the positive uses of social media, as well as its challenges.

How is the workshop structured?

Each team will consist of science communicators, including students under training and professionals, and early-career researchers interested in science communication. We hope that the diversity of experience and expertise will allow for peer-to-peer learning.

Each team will be paired with a different organisation that will benefit from the videos created during the workshop series. The exact nature of the outputs and expectations will be agreed between the organisation and the teams. CancerServe is one of the partnering organisations. We are actively reaching out to science-based charities/organisations to build a diverse portfolio that can be matched with your research expertise and interests.

The teams and the assigned partnering organisations will be announced after registration closes.

How to Register?

Who can apply? Undergraduate, master's, and PhD students, as well as science communicators.
When? November 2026–January 2027, with the mixer session on 14 November 2026 in Oxford. The workshop sessions are conducted online.
Places available Up to 20 participants.
Registration closes on 1st November 2026.

No prior experience in video or film is needed; just bring your enthusiasm!

As this is our first workshop series of its kind, registration numbers will be kept small. We have an exciting line-up of facilitators and sessions, so sign up today.

If you have any questions, please contact us at scomsocimperial@gmail.com or president@ossce.org.uk.
