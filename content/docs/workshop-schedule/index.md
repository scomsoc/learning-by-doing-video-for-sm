We will begin the workshop series with a mixer session on the 14th November 2026. The workshop series includes ‘How to’ workshop sessions, where we introduce the basics of science communication for social media, filming and editing videos. These sessions will be two hours long and online.

A significant chunk of work is self-organised ‘Do It Yourself’ activities, which are crucial to the workshop series' success. Each team is expected to organise at least one meeting with the partnering organisation to understand their needs for creating videos for their social media. After the first set of ‘How to’ workshops, teams will be shooting raw footage for their videos on their own.

As organisers, we recommend that the participants aim to create 2 short-form (30-60 seconds) video pieces of content (Reels, TikTok videos) and one long-form (2-3 min) video for YouTube/website. The nature of the video can vary from awareness-style to interviews and explainers. However, depending on participants' interest and availability, the exact quantity of output can differ.

Finally, we will organise ‘Ponder’ sessions, a more theoretical conversation about social media and science communication. We will also schedule office hours throughout the duration of the workshop series to address questions or challenges you may encounter.
