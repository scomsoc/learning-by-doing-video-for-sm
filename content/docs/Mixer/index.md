## Preparatory work
There are no pre-readings or pre-assignments. However, if you use social media (who doesn’t!), start keeping a note of the science content you see on your feed. This will be useful as you begin thinking about the kinds of videos you might like to create during the workshop.

## Mixer Summary by Organisers

In the mixer, we, the organisers, described the motivations (read more, here) to run the workshop series on making short-form science content for social media. The session also emphaised the key pillars of the workshop: learning-by-doing and peer-to-peer learning. The participants then spent some time getting to know their team members and their chosen charities.
