## Workshop Mixer
:date: When? 14th Nov. 2026, Sat. 1600 - 1800

:round_pushpin: Where? Worcester College, University of Oxford

## Schedule

15:00 - 15:45. An optional Oxford colleges tour.

16:00 - 18:00. Join us for an in-person mixer at Worcester college, University of Oxford, where participants meet each other and (potentially) some representatives from the participating organisations. Participants will also be given more information about the workshop series and about their collaboration with partnering organisations.

18:30 - 20:00 Light snacks at a pub nearby

## Preparatory work
There are no pre-readings or pre-assignments. However, if you use social media (who doesn’t!), start keeping a note of the science content you see on your feed. This will be useful as you begin thinking about the kinds of videos you might like to create during the workshop.
